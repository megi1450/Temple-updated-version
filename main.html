Our temple

Rooted in tradition.
Strengthened by community.

Located within the Arvind Oasis community, Shri Varasiddhi Vinayaka Temple is dedicated to Lord Ganesha and brings residents together through daily worship, special sevas and shared celebrations.

The temple’s journey began with the Bhoomi Puja on 23 November 2023, followed by the 48-day Mandala Puja and Prana Prathishtapana on 14 March 2024.
